Manual zymoBIOMICS-style microbial prep on the bench, single 96-well workflow.

Bring one Sample Plate holding 50 uL of each stool sample in wells A1-D4 to the bench.
Transfer 50 uL from the Sample Plate to the Binding Plate.
Add 700 uL of lysis buffer to each well of the Binding Plate.
Bead beat the Binding Plate for 5 minutes.
Spin the Binding Plate for 10 minutes at 3000 rpm.
Transfer 500 uL from the Binding Plate to the Wash Plate.
Wash the Wash Plate 3 times with 800 uL of wash buffer.
Elute the Wash Plate with 100 uL of elution buffer.
Incubate the Eluate Plate at 37 C for 30 minutes.
Read the Eluate Plate at OD600.
