## Tick mechanics / lessons
- Spec must be written **before** the worktree is created (or copied in): the new worktree is checked
  out from the trunk commit and does not contain untracked spec files.
- The first cl-senior dispatch **wandered off-task** (~24 min, 47 tool calls, no edits): it went
  reading `cl-lane-stack.sh`, `CL_DATA_DIR` handling and the OPS-1 spec instead of doing EDITOR-2, then
  died with an empty log and an unfinalised session row. The retry with a step-numbered, explicitly
  "do not investigate X" contract and `setsid nohup` detachment finished the whole item cleanly.
  Prompt shape matters more than model choice here.

  `setsid nohup … < /dev/null &`.
