## The measured defect (verified by the orchestrator at fd1e496e)

config/drafting/mixture-material-cues.yaml declares cue "/" with
firesOn: [ratioAdjacent, flankingComponents] and flanking minComponents: 2,
minComponentLength: 3, componentPattern "[A-Za-z][A-Za-z-]*", stopWords [with,
without, and, or, plus, in, of, per, for, to, the, a, an].

"DNase/RNase Free Water" contains "/"; the words flanking it — "DNase" (5 chars)
and "RNase" (5 chars) — BOTH match componentPattern, exceed minComponentLength,
and are NOT stopWords. The flanking arm therefore fires: minComponents=2 is
satisfied, and the label bars with `unrealized-mixture-blob-material`. This is
exactly the false-bar class the YAML's own fix1/D1 header forbids ("real,
catalogued SINGLE reagents carry separators and percentages in their names —
DMEM/F12 …"). DMEM/F12 escapes only because "F12" contains a digit and so fails
componentPattern; "RNase" has no digit. The stopWord list has no arm for a label
that is itself ONE catalogued reagent whose name merely contains a separator.
