### The North Star Doctrine

The North Star Doctrine says that no prompt is called DONE until it has browser review.

Computable-lab is currently an impressive pile of parts that don't fit together.  The new workflow is that the app is built by the browser-scout driving the prompts and driving the journeys.

It should read the propmt, imagine wh
at it expects the journey to be then try to walk that path using its vision to see all of the ways that the journey FAILS, with screenshots and a trail of evidence for the srchitect to clean up.
